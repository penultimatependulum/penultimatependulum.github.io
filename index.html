BEHIND THE CANVAS

A little precision.
A lot of possibility.

At Penultimate Pendulum Studio, paintings begin with a simple idea: suspend paint, set it in motion, and see where the movement leads.

The result is a meeting of careful preparation and beautiful unpredictability. Every canvas captures a moment that can't be repeated exactly.

Even the titles have a life of their own.
